# HERO Logistics SaaS — Company Admin Flow & Implementation Document

## Overview
Defines the boundary and operational workflow of the Tenant Transport Company Admin portal.

## Core Responsibilities
1. **Fleet & Driver Operations:** Manage trucks, trailers, drivers, shifts, rosters, and pre-start checklists.
2. **Dispatch & Load Execution:** Control load creation, stops, route optimization, real-time GPS tracking, and delivery sign-offs (POD).
3. **Warehouse & Yard Logistics:** Manage inventory, pallet zones, holding lanes, and scanning.
4. **Freight Invoicing & Carrier Accounting:** Generate customer freight invoices, process driver/contractor pay, track fuel surcharges, and review tenant P&L.
5. **Boundary Constraint:** Company Admins manage only their own company's internal staff and operations. They cannot create or view HERO platform Sales staff or Super Admin system configurations.
